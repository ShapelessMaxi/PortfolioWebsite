	<h1>Inital Technicalt Tests - Jan 2024</h1>
<p>I did some testing with the blob track TOP in TouchDesigner. With it, I can easily detect moving objects, or humans, in a space. The size of the blob inform us of the general distance of the person (smaller blob = further, bigger blob = closer). Although this technique is not 100% accurate, I think it would be sufficient for this project.</p>
<p>For the desintegration effect, I tinkered around and created a patch on TD mimicking a pixel sorting algorithm. There is still work to do on this as I am not supr happy with it, but this is the type of effect I am thinking about for the progressive disintegration part.</p>
	
	<h1>Inital Concept - Jan 2024</h1>
<p>Are you afraid of being seen?</p>
<p>In her essay titled Shifting Futures: Digital Trans of Color Praxis, Micha Cardénas writes about the violence committed against specifically black trans women in the US (https://scalar.usc.edu/works/shifting-futures-micha-cardenas/index). She argues that visibility politics made it possible for a black trans movement to gain so much traction. On the other hand, being able to “pass” as cis women is often what saves them from violent acts. Cardénas conceive of this ability to shift through states of visibility as an essential tool for survival. In addition to that, my own experience as a queer subject made me realize that I pretty much only receive violence and slurs when I dress and act more queer, gay, and fem.</p>
<p>What if technology evolved to have human characteristics?</p>
<p>What if digital characters also had anxiety about being visible?</p>
<p>For this piece, I want to depict a scene of queer intimacy. On screen, we would see a gay couple looking at each other longingly. While sensing human presence in front of the screen through camera technology, the image will progressively disintegrate.</p>
<h1>State 1: Intimacy</h1>
<p>the two characters are looking at each other.</p>
<h1>State 2: Consciousness</h1>
<p>the characters’ eyes are tracking the viewer. If there are multiple viewers, their eyes erratically change targets.</p>
<h1>State 3: Progressive disintegration</h1>
<p>pixel sorting algorithm/effect rendering the image progressively harder to read.</p>
<p>The digital couple would be 3D modelled, textured and animated in Blender and Substance Painter.</p>
<p>The conscious and interactive part would be programmed in TouchDesigner, using the Blob tracking technique and/or MediaPipe. I am also intrigued by IR cameras (night vision) as a way to get more precise images to analyze.</p>
